# Enterprise AI Practices | Expertise Coaching
## Six tracks · 42 topics · Three enterprise practices
**Leads Track (4 weeks each)** followed by **Engineers Track (10 weeks each)**
Each topic: **90 min facilitated session + 45 min Q&A + 45 min timed capstone**.
Final-week 45-minute capstones are live defenses of cumulative work built across the program.
